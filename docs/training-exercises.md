# Training: starting exercise list

The exercises the training section starts with (stage 1). Nick can add more later on the Exercise list screen. Names follow one style: Title Case, equipment first where it matters, singular, and hyphenated words capitalised on both sides.

Exercises without a listed type are **Weight** (reps × kg). The type decides what's logged:

- **Weight:** reps and kg.
- **Bodyweight:** reps only.
- **Band:** band (free text, for example "Red" or "No band") and reps.
- **Timed:** seconds.

Groups are only for reading this list. The app doesn't group exercises.
