## Method

1. Preheat oven to 200°C. Line two large baking trays with baking paper.

2. Combine harissa paste, olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, salt and pepper. Score each chicken breast lightly with a knife, then coat thoroughly in the marinade. Set aside for 10 minutes.

3. Place chicken on one tray. Bake for 24–28 minutes until cooked through (internal temp 74°C). Tent with foil and rest for 5 minutes before slicing.

4. While chicken bakes, pat chickpeas dry with paper towel (this helps them crisp). Toss chickpeas and capsicum strips with olive oil, smoked paprika, cumin, coriander, chilli flakes, salt and pepper. Spread in a single layer on the second tray.

5. Roast at 210°C for 25–28 minutes, stirring once, until chickpeas are golden and capsicum edges are slightly charred.

6. Slice chicken across the grain. Cool everything completely before portioning into 2 containers — one chicken breast portion and half the chickpea-capsicum mixture per container.

7. Seal and refrigerate. Keeps 4 days in the fridge.

**Reheat:** Air fry at 185°C for 8–10 minutes. No need to add oil or liquid.
