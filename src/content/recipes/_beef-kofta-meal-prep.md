## Method

1. Preheat oven to 210°C. Line two large baking trays with baking paper.

2. Combine beef mince, grated onion, garlic, parsley, cumin, coriander, paprika, cinnamon, salt and pepper in a bowl. Mix until well combined.

3. Divide into 12 equal portions. Shape each into an oval kofta roughly 8cm long. Place on one tray.

4. Bake for 20–22 minutes until browned and cooked through, turning once at the halfway mark.

5. While kofta bakes, toss eggplant, zucchini, green beans and carrot with olive oil, cumin, smoked paprika, salt and pepper. Spread in a single layer on the second tray — use two trays if crowded.

6. Roast veg at 210°C for 25–28 minutes, turning once, until edges are caramelised.

7. Cool completely before portioning into 3 containers — 4 kofta and one portion of roasted veg per container.

8. Seal and refrigerate. Keeps 4 days in the fridge.

**Reheat:** Air fry at 190°C for 8–10 minutes. No need to add oil or liquid.
