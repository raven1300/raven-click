## Method

1. Preheat oven to 200°C. Line two large baking trays with baking paper.

2. Combine smoked paprika, garlic powder, oregano, cumin, 2 tbsp olive oil, lemon juice, salt and pepper into a paste. Coat chicken thighs thoroughly and set aside for 10 minutes.

3. Arrange chicken on one tray in a single layer. Bake for 28–32 minutes until cooked through and edges are caramelised. Do not add any liquid.

4. While chicken bakes, toss capsicum, zucchini and red onion with 2 tbsp olive oil, smoked paprika, oregano, garlic, salt and pepper. Spread on the second tray in a single layer — do not crowd, use two trays if needed.

5. Roast veg at 210°C for 30 minutes, turning once at the halfway point. Add cherry tomatoes in the last 8 minutes.

6. Rest chicken for 5 minutes. Cool everything completely before portioning into 3 containers — one chicken thigh (approx. 150–160g cooked) and one portion of roasted veg per container.

7. Seal and refrigerate. Keeps 4 days in the fridge.

**Reheat:** Air fry at 180°C for 8–10 minutes. No need to add oil or liquid.
