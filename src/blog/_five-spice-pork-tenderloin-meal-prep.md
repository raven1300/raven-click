## Method

1. Preheat oven to 200°C. Line a large baking tray with baking paper.

2. Combine five-spice, soy sauce, honey, garlic, ginger and sesame oil into a marinade. Coat pork tenderloin thoroughly and marinate for 10 minutes.

3. Place pork on the tray and roast for 22–25 minutes until cooked through (internal temp 65°C) and exterior is caramelised. Rest for 5 minutes before slicing.

4. While pork rests, toss broccolini, cabbage and carrot with sesame oil, soy sauce, rice wine vinegar and garlic powder. Spread on a second lined tray.

5. Roast veg at 210°C for 18–20 minutes, turning once, until edges char slightly and cabbage is tender. Scatter sesame seeds over before the final 3 minutes.

6. Slice pork into 1.5cm rounds. Cool everything completely before portioning into 2 containers — equal pork portions and one portion of Asian veg per container.

7. Seal and refrigerate. Keeps 4 days in the fridge.

**Reheat:** Air fry at 185°C for 7–9 minutes. No need to add oil or liquid.
