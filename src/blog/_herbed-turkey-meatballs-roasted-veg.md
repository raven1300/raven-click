## Method

1. Preheat oven to 200°C. Line two large baking trays with baking paper.

2. Combine turkey mince, egg, breadcrumbs, garlic, parsley, oregano, thyme, onion powder, salt and pepper in a bowl. Mix until just combined — do not overwork.

3. Roll into 18–20 meatballs (approx. 30g each). Place on one tray and brush lightly with olive oil.

4. Bake for 20–22 minutes until golden and cooked through (internal temp 74°C). Do not flip — the base will form a crust.

5. While meatballs bake, toss broccoli, cauliflower and sweet potato with olive oil, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper. Spread in a single layer on the second tray.

6. Roast veg at 200°C for 28–30 minutes, turning once at the halfway mark, until edges are golden and sweet potato is tender.

7. Cool everything completely before portioning into 3 containers — 6 meatballs and one portion of roasted veg per container.

8. Seal and refrigerate. Keeps 4 days in the fridge.

**Reheat:** Air fry at 180°C for 8–10 minutes. No need to add oil or liquid.
